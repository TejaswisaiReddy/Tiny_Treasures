How I work.

A thoughtful process that combines listening, visual exploration, structured design, and hands-on prototyping.

  1. 01

    Listen first

    I use surveys and interviews to understand people's needs, frustrations, and existing behaviours before deciding what to design. In Tiny Treasures, I gathered input from 25 parents; in Pickde, I surveyed 78 people.

  2. 02

    Sketch on paper

    I use quick sketches to explore layouts and alternative ideas before committing to detailed screens. Drawing helps me work through possibilities early, when changes are still easy to make.

  3. 03

    Design in systems

    Reusable components, consistent patterns and WCAG 2.2 checks — habits I built inside Nokia’s design system.

  4. 04

    Prototype and build

    I turn flows into clickable Figma prototypes, and use AI coding tools to build front-end prototypes people can click through.
